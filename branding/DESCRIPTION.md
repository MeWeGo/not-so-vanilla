# Not So Vanilla

**Remember your first night in Minecraft?**

You didn't know how to make a bed. The dark was terrifying. Every noise behind you was a zombie. A village on the horizon felt like a whole adventure, and the world felt endless.

**Not So Vanilla** tries to give you that feeling back. It still looks and plays like Minecraft, but the world is harder, bigger and less forgiving, and you'll have to learn how it works all over again.

---

## 🌑 The night is scary again

- **Darkness is truly dark.** On a moonless night or deep in a cave, you can't see a thing without a torch.
- **Mobs hear you.** Sprinting, mining and fighting make noise, and nearby monsters come to check it out. Sneak, hide, and stay quiet.
- **Sound bounces off the world.** Caves echo, and walls muffle sound. You'll hear things before you see them.
- **You're not alone out there.** Bandits roam the wilds with swords and bows. Survivors look exactly like them. You won't know who's friendly until it's too late.

## 🩸 Getting hurt actually matters

- **Every limb has its own health.** Your head, chest, arms and legs can each get hurt. Lose your head or chest and you die.
- **Bleeding, fractures and pain.** A broken leg slows you down, and a bleed won't stop by itself.
- **Real treatment.** Craft bandages, tourniquets, splints, painkillers and surgery kits. Drag them onto the injured limb in your inventory, Tarkov-style. Treatment takes time, and getting hit stops it.
- **A field medicine book** with illustrated recipes is waiting in your inventory when you first spawn. You'll need it.

## 💧 Survival is more than a hunger bar

- **Thirst.** Drink only clean water. Fill a bowl or a bottle from a river, then boil it on a campfire before you drink it, or you'll get sick. Seawater is no good.
- **Temperature.** Deserts burn and mountains freeze. Dress for the weather, find shelter and light a fire.
- **Seasons.** Spring, summer, autumn and winter change the weather, crops and temperature. Winter is long and cold, so prepare.
- **Longer days.** A full day lasts about 40 minutes, and summer days are longer than winter ones.
- **Food variety.** Eating the same thing every day gets you nowhere. Cook different meals to grow stronger. Carry your food in a lunchbag.
- **Weight.** Everything you carry has a real weight. Overload yourself and you'll slow down, but you'll also train your Strength.
- **Slower walking.** The world feels bigger when you can't sprint across it in a minute.

## 🗺️ A world worth exploring

- **New biomes** everywhere: huge mountains, canyons, forests and more.
- **Towers, taverns, dungeons and villages** to find, at vanilla spacing. Climb a hill and you'll spot something worth walking to.
- **See for miles.** Distant Horizons renders terrain and buildings far past your normal view distance.
- **Living villages.** Villagers are real people with names, families, jobs and moods. You can talk to them, trade with them and befriend them.
- **More life:** new animals, fishing, fruit trees, new mobs and more natural-looking landscapes.

## ✨ Looks and feels great

- **Shaders included** (Complementary Reimagined), with northern lights on full-moon nights and a sky full of stars, plus a smooth, natural-feeling camera and 3D skin layers.
- **Better animations** and on-screen damage effects. Getting hit, and getting blood on your screen, feels like it.
- **Performance mods built in**, so it runs well despite everything above.

## 🤝 Play with friends

- **Works on servers.** Host a dedicated server for the group.
- **Or just open your world to friends.** e4mc gives you a link anyone can join over the internet. No port forwarding, no VPN.

## ⚙️ Requirements

- **Minecraft 26.2** with **NeoForge**
- **RAM:** at least **6 GB** for the game, **8 GB** recommended (Distant Horizons and shaders like memory)
- A graphics card that can handle shaders. You can turn shaders off in Options → Video Settings → Shader Packs.

## 💡 Tips for your first night

1. Open the **Field Medicine** book. Learn the bandage recipe before you need it.
2. Build a **campfire** early. You'll need it for clean water, cooking and warmth.
3. **Don't sprint at night.** Monsters will hear you.
4. That stranger in leather armour might be friendly… or not. If one of them is kind, offer them a good meal. They might give you medicine in return.
5. Press **R** or **U** on an item to see its recipe or what it's used in (JEI).

---

*Not So Vanilla is a hard pack. You'll die a lot at first. That's the point. You're 9 again.*
